Generate questions

Claude drafts the questions, then they are validated into the four-option format before being stored. This takes minutes, not seconds — you can leave this page open and it will report when done.

Hold Ctrl (or Cmd) to pick several. The badges scope what the questions may test. Each finished question is then reviewed against the whole catalog and also filed under any other badge it genuinely tests.
Preferred over anything Claude finds on its own. This is where internal training content goes — the server cannot reach Glean or a cluster itself.
