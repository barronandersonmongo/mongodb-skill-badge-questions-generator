Generate questions

A run walks the documentation a badge is about one chunk at a time — a heading and the passage under it, not a whole page — and writes several questions from each. A page is a few chunks on average, and chunks already written from are skipped, so running a badge again covers new material rather than repeating. Several badges are walked one after another, each getting the full budget below.

Hold Ctrl (or Cmd) to pick several. The badges scope what the questions may test. Each finished question is then reviewed against the whole catalog and also filed under any other badge it genuinely tests.
